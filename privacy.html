Privacy Statement

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Who we are

Cognitif Group Limited ("CGL", "we", "us") is a company registered in England & Wales, Company No. 17154680, registered office 128 City Road, London, United Kingdom, EC1V 2NX. We are registered with the Information Commissioner (ICO) under registration number ZC 173064.

2. This website does not collect personal data

This website does not use cookies, does not run analytics or tracking scripts, and does not use forms. No personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted by this website.

3. Data Controller status during Petalyx™ and HMX sessions

Cognitif Group Limited acts as a Data Controller only during each Petalyx™ or HMX session. During a session, the following is processed on the User's device:

  • the response words chosen;
  • the response words not chosen; and
  • how long each response takes.

Individual choices and response times are destroyed on the User's device at the end of the session. Except in cases where data is consensually archived for training purposes, no personal data is retained beyond that session, and the role of Data Controller does not endure beyond the end of each Petalyx™ or HMX session.

4. This website: no sharing, no international transfers

Because this website does not collect personal data, none is shared with any third party, and none is transferred outside the UK.

5. Lawful basis: legitimate interest, not consent

For Petalyx™ or HMX sessions, Cognitif Group Limited processes data solely on the basis of its legitimate interest as a market research analytical platform, under UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f). We have assessed that this interest is not overridden by the interests or fundamental rights of the individuals concerned.

In instances where you consent to the use of input data for internal training purposes, CGL continues to act as Data Controller until the earlier of (a) your request for the data to be deleted or (b) 3 calendar years from the date of capture. No data captured in this way will be shared with 3rd parties except as required by Law.

Our services are built to produce aggregate, population-level measurement, not to identify, profile, or make decisions about specific individuals. Clients and Users may only access our services for lawful, legitimate purposes consistent with this design, and are not authorised to access them, or combine output with other data, to re-identify or single out an individual.

6. Your rights

You have the right to object to processing under UK GDPR Article 21. You can exercise this at any time by not starting, or by leaving, a Petalyx™ session. Because nothing that identifies you is kept after a session ends, most other rights (such as access or erasure) cannot apply afterwards: there is no record of you for us to find.

You have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or by telephone on 0303 123 1113. For any other query, email andrew@cognitifgroup.ai.

7. Changes to this statement

We may update this statement from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
